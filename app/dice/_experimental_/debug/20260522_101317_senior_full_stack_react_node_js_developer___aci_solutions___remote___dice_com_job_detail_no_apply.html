Summary

ACI Solutions is currently seeking a highly skilled Senior Full Stack React/Node.js Developer to join our technology team. This role is ideal for an experienced developer who thrives in a fast-paced environment, enjoys solving complex software challenges, and is passionate about building scalable, cloud-native applications.

Position Responsibilities

  • Design, develop, test, and maintain scalable full-stack applications using React and Node.js
  • Develop and support cloud-native solutions leveraging AWS services
  • Collaborate with business stakeholders and technical teams to translate requirements into technical solutions
  • Troubleshoot application and system issues, identify root causes, and implement long-term solutions
  • Build and maintain APIs, integrations, and backend services
  • Participate in code reviews and contribute to software development best practices
  • Support CI/CD pipelines and DevOps initiatives
  • Optimize application performance, scalability, and security
  • Contribute to Agile ceremonies, sprint planning, and technical discussions

Required Qualifications

  • Minimum of 5 years of professional experience developing applications with React
  • Minimum of 5 years of professional experience developing applications with Node.js
  • Minimum of 3 years of professional experience working within AWS environments, including:
    • Lambda
    • DynamoDB
    • S3
    • EventBridge
    • Kinesis
  • Strong understanding of complex business processes and the systems supporting them
  • Ability to analyze technical problems, identify root causes, and implement effective solutions
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills
  • Ability to collaborate effectively in a team-oriented environment
  • Passion for solving complex software challenges and building innovative solutions
  • Bachelor’s degree required

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with Single Page Application (SPA) concepts and frameworks
  • Experience working in Agile and DevOps environments
  • Familiarity with:
    • Git
    • Jenkins
    • CloudBees
    • React Testing Library
    • Jest
  • Experience with relational databases and DynamoDB strongly preferred
